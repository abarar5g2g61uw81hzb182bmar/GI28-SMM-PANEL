Why it developed?

I am the founder of GI28. There was a time when I was in urgent need of money, but I had no source of income. Even investing in oneself requires money—something that some people receive from their background, but many do not.

At the age of 16, I not only learned web development but also understood a business strategy. I started this platform from ₹0 and gradually earned money through it, reinvesting to upgrade and grow the platform.
